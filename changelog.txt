Version 1.0.3 (2026-11-09)
- Fixed: Fast survey was offered on planets where the normal survey is blocked, such as a planet guarded by an Industrial Evolution artillery platform. It now only appears where Perform a survey is possible.

Version 1.0.2 (2026-11-09)
- Fixed: after a salvage find with its own buttons (such as a credit deposit), those buttons stayed on screen next to Continue, and clicking one again showed an error. Sometimes there were two Continue options. Both fixed; the dialog now goes straight on to the salvage cost screen.

Version 1.0.1 (2026-11-08)
- The mod now links to its forum thread, so version checkers can point you there.

Version 1.0.0 (2026-11-08)
- First stable release. No gameplay changes since 0.3.0.

Version 0.3.0 (2026-11-08)
- New: skip the Continue after a salvage find that asks nothing.
- New: skip the Continue after beating automated defenses.
- New: unlocked cargo pods open straight to their contents.
- Changed: the salvage skips no longer override other mods' rules on the same screens (Secrets of the Frontier, Content Unlocking Missions and others).

Version 0.2.0 (2026-11-08)
