[center][b]
WHAT DOES THIS MOD DO?
You survey about five planets in every system, and you salvage everything that floats.
Each of those takes a stack of screens where the only button is "Continue".
SkipClicks cuts the screens that never ask you anything, and keeps every screen where you choose something.
Needs LunaLib. PatchLib is optional (only for the after-battle part).
[/b][/center]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Surveying a planet[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[b]Without SkipClicks:[/b]
[list type=decimal]
[li]Open the planet.[/li]
[li]Perform a survey. The survey screen opens with the cost.[/li]
[li]Perform survey. A short animation shows the planet's features.[/li]
[li]Continue. Back to the planet, now with Establish a colony and Leave.[/li]
[li]Leave, back to the planet's main screen.[/li]
[li]Leave again.[/li]
[/list]
[b]With SkipClicks:[/b]
[list type=decimal]
[li]Open the planet.[/li]
[li]Fast survey. The cost is paid, the survey runs, and the dialog shows the survey data, the conditions and the experience. Explore the ruins and Establish a colony are right there.[/li]
[/list]
[list]
[li]The Fast survey option shows the supplies it uses. It is greyed out, with the missing crew, heavy machinery or supplies in the tooltip, when your fleet lacks them.[/li]
[li]Same cost, same survey data and same experience as a normal survey. The normal Perform a survey option is still there.[/li]
[li]Optional: close the planet dialog after a fast survey when there are no ruins to explore.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Salvaging[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[b]Without SkipClicks:[/b]
[list type=decimal]
[li]Scavenge (or Nexerelin's auto-scavenge). The risk screen opens with Assess and Leave.[/li]
[li]Assess. Now you see the requirements and the salvage effectiveness, with Begin salvage operations.[/li]
[li]Begin salvage operations.[/li]
[li]Sometimes an accident: a damage report with only Continue.[/li]
[li]The loot screen.[/li]
[li]A station, probe or cache then turns into a debris field, and you do it all again: scavenge, Assess, Begin salvage operations, loot.[/li]
[/list]
[b]With SkipClicks:[/b]
[list type=decimal]
[li]Scavenge. You land on the requirements screen right away.[/li]
[li]Begin salvage operations.[/li]
[li]The loot screen, with the leftover debris field's loot already in it.[/li]
[/list]
[list]
[li]Debris fields skip Assess. Derelicts, stations and caches skip Explore.[/li]
[li]Never skipped when automated defenses guard the target, so you can still leave before the fight. After you beat the defenses, no more screen with only Continue.[/li]
[li]After a salvage find that asks nothing of you (survey data, a blueprint, a hint about another wreck, cargo), the dialog goes on to the requirements screen. The find stays in the dialog text. Finds with a choice, such as survivors, still ask.[/li]
[li]A salvage accident still costs what it costs. The loss goes to the message log and the loot screen opens right away.[/li]
[li]The leftover debris gives the same loot and experience as scavenging it yourself, and no debris field is left behind. Demolishing a station still leaves its debris field.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Cargo pods[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[b]Without SkipClicks:[/b] open the pods, then Examine pod contents, then the contents.
[b]With SkipClicks:[/b] open the pods, then the contents.
[list]
[li]Unlocked pods only. When you close the contents, the usual options are back (such as putting the pods into a stable orbit).[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]After a battle[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[b]Without SkipClicks:[/b] Continue on the ship recovery screen, then a screen with only Pick through the wreckage, then the loot.
[b]With SkipClicks (and PatchLib):[/b] Continue on the ship recovery screen, then the loot.
[list]
[li]Credits found in the wreckage go to the message log.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Settings and compatibility[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Every skip has its own switch in the LunaLib settings.[/li]
[li]Every skip steps aside when another mod has its own quest, reward or options on that screen.[/li]
[li]Works with Nexerelin's battle screen.[/li]
[li]Safe to add to or remove from a game in progress.[/li]
[li][url=https://github.com/Lukas22041/PatchLib]PatchLib[/url] is only needed for the after-battle skip. Everything else works without it.[/li]
[/list]
