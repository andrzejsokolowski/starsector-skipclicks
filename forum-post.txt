[center][b]
WHAT DOES THIS MOD DO?
Surveying a planet, salvaging a wreck and looting after a battle each take several screens of "Continue".
SkipClicks cuts the ones that never ask you anything.
Needs LunaLib. PatchLib is optional (only for the after-battle part).
[/b][/center]
[center]
[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Planet survey[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Unsurveyed planets with no colony get a [b]Fast survey[/b] option. It shows the supplies it uses.[/li]
[li]One click pays the survey cost and runs the full survey. No survey screen, no animation, no Continue.[/li]
[li]The planet dialog shows the survey data you got, the planet's conditions and the experience, with Establish a colony and Explore the ruins ready to pick.[/li]
[li]Same cost, same survey data and same experience as a normal survey. The normal Perform a survey option is still there.[/li]
[li]Greyed out, with the missing crew, heavy machinery or supplies in the tooltip, when your fleet lacks them.[/li]
[li]Optional: close the planet dialog after a fast survey when there are no ruins to explore.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Salvage[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Debris fields skip Assess, and derelicts, stations and caches skip Explore. You land on the salvage cost screen with Begin salvage operations.[/li]
[li]Never skipped when automated defenses guard the target, so you can still leave before the fight.[/li]
[li]When a salvage accident costs crew or heavy machinery, the loot screen opens right away. The losses go to the message log.[/li]
[li]After a find that asks nothing of you (survey data, a blueprint, a hint about another wreck, cargo), the dialog goes straight on to the salvage cost screen. The find stays in the dialog text.[/li]
[li]After you beat the automated defenses, no more screen with only Continue.[/li]
[li]Salvaging a station, probe, cache or other derelict no longer leaves a debris field to scavenge afterwards. The debris loot and its experience join the first loot screen.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Cargo pods[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Unlocked cargo pods show their contents right away, without the Examine pod contents step. The usual options are there after you close it.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]After battle[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]With PatchLib installed: Continue on the ship recovery screen opens the loot screen right away, instead of a screen with only Pick through the wreckage.[/li]
[li]Credits found in the wreckage go to the message log.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Settings and compatibility[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Every skip has its own switch in the LunaLib settings.[/li]
[li]Every skip steps aside when another mod has its own quest, reward or options on that screen.[/li]
[li]Works with Nexerelin's battle screen.[/li]
[li]Safe to add to or remove from a game in progress.[/li]
[/list]
[/center]
